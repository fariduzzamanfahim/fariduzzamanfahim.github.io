About

I'm MD Fahim, a first-semester Computer Science and Engineering student at Southeast University. I'm early in my degree, but I already know the direction I want to take it: offensive security.

What draws me to red teaming is the mindset — thinking like an adversary to find weaknesses before they're exploited for real. Right now that means building the fundamentals properly: how networks actually work, how operating systems behave under the hood, and how to write code that automates the boring parts of an attack.

This site is where I'll track that path in public — what I'm learning, what I'm building, and eventually, the work that comes out of it.
